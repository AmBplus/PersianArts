## Best three single references to study

**1. `mort1d__demo.mp4` (Mort1d/motion-graphics-skills) - motion quality.** It is a finished, code-rendered reel rather than a still, so it shows the only thing a still cannot: how a shot actually *moves* - entry/exit easing, hold length, whether the camera moves or the layout breathes, and how many frames a beat is allowed to occupy. `mort1d__demo.gif` is the same material in a loopable GIF, useful for scrubbing a single beat repeatedly without a video scrubber.

**2. `bang__12-more-combinations.jpg` (bangtutorial/bang-motion) - typography and art direction.** The gallery is twelve stills of one system re-skinned twelve ways (grainy gradient, kinetic colorblock, flat vector, saas ui tour, continuous action, cartoon stage, flat pop photo, white catalog, visual journalism, vintage sketch, cartoon collage), so the pair `bang__02-kinetic-colorblock.jpg` + `bang__12-more-combinations.jpg` is a compact style bible: type scale, weight, tracking, corner radius, and palette decisions side by side. `bang__09-visual-journalism.jpg` and `bang__10-vintage-sketch.jpg` are the two best for Persian text because both rely on a strong single-family hierarchy rather than baked raster lettering.

**3. `motionmaxxing__spotify-comparison.mp4` (Tejashmakwana/motionmaxxing) - compositing.** It is real product-page motion: UI panels, captions and glow layered over each other in a moving frame, which is the hard part the stills cannot teach. `motionmaxxing__hero.gif` shows the same discipline in a seamless loop, and the 40 `talkcraft__*.png` thumbnails are a shot-by-shot catalogue of compositing devices (split compare slider, gooey morph, black slam transition, filmstrip conveyor, rack focus pair) that can be copied one at a time. The `lemo__*.jpg` styleframes are the fallback palette/finish reference when a Persian shot needs a distinct look.
