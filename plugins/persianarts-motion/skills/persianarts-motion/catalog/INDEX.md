# Ingredient catalog

Generated by `pa catalog` from every ingredient's `meta` (engine and installed packs). **Empty in v0.1** — phase 1
adds the first ingredients (from shotcraft cards `paper-title-card`, `list-reveal`, `marker-underline-title`,
`radial-wave`). Until then, the approved Persian shotcraft cards in the PersianArts repo
(`work/persian/src/demos/<category>/<slug>/`) can be studied as references for motion ideas, but they are not
themable yet: rebuild what you need as a project ingredient (`references/craft/cooking.md`).
