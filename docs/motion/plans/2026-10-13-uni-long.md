## Decisions
D1: Concept "From a dot to wings" (one continuous line, no slideshow). Rejected: "The gate" (needs a hi-res campus photo), "Pencil checklist" (list, reads as slideshow). Evidence: `uni-creative-direction.md` section 1.
D2: Grid 48 beats at 120 BPM reference (`CUT_BEATS` in `uni/motion.ts`). Rejected: free timing. Evidence: `uni-creative-direction.md` one-page summary.
D3: Hit grammar (anticipation, hit on beat frame, squash) defined before build. Evidence: `uni-creative-direction.md` section 3.
D4: Short 12 s test cut built first, 30 s later. Rejected: building 30 s first. Evidence: `uni-creative-direction.md` section 10.6 and 11.

## Events / hit contract
Defined in the creative document, not as a code contract: a per-scene HIT list with frame numbers and sound per hit (`uni-creative-direction.md` scene section and the frame table near line 165). The code has no typed hit/event interface; hits are literal frame constants inside `LongFilm.tsx` (`SC = [0, 90, 195, 330, 480, 630, 750, 900]`, 15-frame grid, 30 fps).

- The planning record does not show `DECISIONS.jsonl` being checked; no decision line there covers this film.
- The 30 s film timeline is 900 frames at 30 fps (`SC` last cut 900), but the creative document uses a 24 s schedule (sec 10.3); the mismatch is not resolved in this plan.
