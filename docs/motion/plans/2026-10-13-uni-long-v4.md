| Scene | Archetype | Composition | Type | Motion grammar | Palette | Transition INTO next | Hand-made detail |

| S7 750-900 | emblem + CTA card | split: blue emblem block / cream card | Vazirmatn 900 navy, LTR address, gold CTA button | build then settle, last beat CTA press | cream + navy + gold | (end) | the S1 brush returns to underline the CTA |
